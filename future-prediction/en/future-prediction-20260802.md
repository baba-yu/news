Four situations are covered and all four are disclosure: a system that interacts directly with a person has to say it is an AI, synthetic image, audio, video and text has to carry machine-readable marks so it can be detected, deployers of emotion-recognition and biometric-categorisation systems have to tell the people exposed to them, and deepfakes and AI-generated text on matters of public interest have to be labelled.

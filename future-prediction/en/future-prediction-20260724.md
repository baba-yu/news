The user's second standing prediction (cloud APIs for advanced work plus local LLMs for daily, driven by SaaS price rises) gets both legs printing hard and its price engine running backwards more visibly than on almost any day this cycle. The advanced-cloud leg is the loudest part of the slate: Alphabet raised full-year capital expenditure guidance to $195-205 billion from $180-190 billion after spending $44.9 billion in a single quarter, with Google Cloud up 82% and cloud backlog at $514 billion, and the stock fell after hours anyway; Intel's Data Center and AI segment grew 59% to $6.3 billion inside a quarter that grew 25% overall, its fastest in more than fifteen years, with 18A-P in risk production; SK hynix's board approved 7.09 trillion won for the P&T7 advanced packaging plant at Cheongju, running to the end of 2032, explicitly to secure capacity for AI memory; and Stripe is negotiating near $10 billion for OpenRouter, roughly eight times the $1.3 billion mark it carried out of May, which is a bet that value in hosted inference accrues to the switch above the endpoints rather than to any endpoint. The daily-local leg advanced substantively rather than by rhyme. Unsloth's second non-NVIDIA backend landed four days after its first, Intel XPU support reaching Studio alongside DoRA, a full KV-cache dtype list, per-GPU GGUF selection and an engine fix preferring rocBLAS over hipBLASLt on ROCm RDNA to dodge a Composable Kernel stall; bitsandbytes 0.50.0 shipped a ROCm fused 4-bit SIMT GEMM and Windows ARM64 NEON wheels beside new CUDA 4-bit kernels; KTransformers added LoRA and hybrid fine-tuning for sparse mixture-of-experts models through LLaMA-Factory at roughly 400 tokens per second full fine-tune and 600 with LoRA on Qwen3-30B-A3B across two EPYC 9355 sockets and two RTX 5090s; Ollama cut memory use on Linux integrated GPUs while adding B200 support; and llama.cpp landed four-bit activation quantization on CUDA. The user's third standing prediction (RL/LLM-based forecasting performance improvement, with substrate, governance and real-world deployment legs hardening in parallel) is the rare case where all three legs got something, including the one that has been blank for weeks. The substrate leg advanced on sparse-backbone training and long-context serving together: KTransformers brought LoRA and hybrid fine-tuning for sparse mixture-of-experts models onto two commodity RTX 5090s beside two server sockets, unsloth-zoo unstacked the Hugging Face-to-vLLM mapper so in-memory LoRA works on vLLM 0.25.0 and later - the weight-transfer plumbing a reinforcement-learning rollout loop actually runs on - and DeepSeek's cutover leaves v4-pro at 1.6 trillion total parameters with 49 billion active and v4-flash at 284 billion with 13 billion active, both on a million-token context, which is the mixture-of-experts-plus-ultra-long-context substrate this leg tracks. The governance leg got its most concrete draft yet in the AI Kill Switch Act's graduated response - changing inference rates or compute allocation, restricting a single capability, suspending a system, shutting it down or forcing a fallback to an earlier model version - closer to the enterprise-grade procurement perimeter an autonomous forecasting agent would have to run inside than anything shipped this month, though aimed at loss-of-control rather than at attestation, and Anthropic's disclosure of a per-domain classifier setting quoted as a percentage against a sibling model treats capability gating as a tunable product surface while the twenty-five-name open-weights letter argues in the opposite direction against premature restriction. Substrate reinforced on two fronts, governance drafted rather than shipped, and a deployment datapoint after a long silence, with IEEE-RAS Humanoids in Santa Clara on December 7-9 the next scheduled place the last leg could firm up.

On the mainstream developer/data side, Apple's WWDC26 runs through June 12 with this week's technical sessions on the new Siri and Foundation Models APIs; the Databricks Data + AI Summit follows June 15-18 at Moscone (30,000-plus in person, 800-plus sessions), per [10times - Databricks Data + AI Summit (Jun 2026), San Francisco](https://10times.com/e1z1-9r17-ppzk); and the AI Engineer World's Fair runs June 28-July 2 at Moscone West with 29 tracks across 6,000-plus attendees, including dedicated Inference (quantization, speculative decoding, KV-cache) and Security tracks, per [AI Engineer World's Fair 2026 - Yerba Buena](https://yerbabuena.org/do/ai-engineer-worlds-fair-2026). On the AI-security track, the Confidential Computing Summit lands June 23-24 at the San Francisco Mint, organized by the Linux Foundation and OPAQUE around confidential AI, attested inference and hardware-verified attestation, per [Linux Foundation - Confidential Computing Summit 2026](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/confidential-computing-summit/); SecurityWeek's AI Risk Summit follows August 11-12 at the Ritz-Carlton Half Moon Bay; and in Las Vegas, Black Hat USA's single-day AI Summit (August 4, inside the August 1-6 conference) and DEF CON 34's AI Village anchor the agentic red-teaming calendar, with OWASP Global AppSec USA in San Francisco November 2-6. Zenity - the practitioner-led AI Agent Security Summit the user tracks - has confirmed a 2026 global expansion to New York, London and APAC, with dates still worth monitoring. On the AI-chip and hardware track, AMD's Advancing AI 2026 event is slated for July and expected to detail availability for the Instinct MI400/MI455X data-center GPU lineup; Hot Chips 2026 takes Stanford August 23-25; and the AI Infra Summit runs September 15-17 at the Santa Clara Convention Center (8,000 attendees, 400-plus speakers across compute, AI data centers and physical AI), per [Information Matters - AI Infra Summit 2026](https://informationmatters.net/event/ai-infra-summit-2026/). Further out, GitHub Universe returns to Fort Mason October 28-29 and AWS re:Invent runs November 30-December 4 in Las Vegas.

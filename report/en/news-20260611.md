### Google ships DiffusionGemma, the first major open-weight text-diffusion model, generating 256-token blocks in parallel for up to 4x faster local inference - with an Unsloth Sudoku fine-tune showing off its bidirectional attention

- On June 10 Google released DiffusionGemma under Apache 2.0, an experimental 26B open-weight model (3.8B active, Mixture-of-Experts) that abandons autoregressive token-by-token decoding for text diffusion: it starts from a canvas of placeholder tokens and refines a 256-token block in parallel over multiple passes, locking in confident tokens as context and finalizing roughly 15-20 per pass, for up to 4x faster generation than a comparable autoregressive baseline, per [Google - DiffusionGemma: 4x faster text generation](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/diffusion-gemma-faster-text-generation/). Google is explicit about the tradeoff - it states DiffusionGemma's output quality is lower than standard Gemma 4 and recommends the autoregressive model for production-quality work, positioning the diffusion variant for speed-critical, low-concurrency local inference rather than high-QPS cloud serving.

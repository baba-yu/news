### Commerce OIG report names four NIST failures driving the NVD backlog past 27,000 unprocessed vulnerabilities

- A US Department of Commerce Office of Inspector General report published today June 1 details how the National Institute of Standards and Technology failed to manage the National Vulnerability Database through the contract gap from February 2024 (when NIST's enrichment contractor support ended) to November 2024 (when a replacement contractor was finally secured), allowing the backlog of unprocessed vulnerabilities to balloon past 27,000 by end-2025; projected 2026 CVE volume is over 60,000 — a near tenfold increase from a decade ago. The OIG names four problems: no formal NVD strategy, duplicate severity scoring on 80% of submissions that already carry scores, redundant CISA / NIST enrichment programs operating without coordination, and stakeholder updates that lag significantly behind operational reality. NIST has until July 25, 2026 to submit a formal action plan, per [Help Net Security - How NIST fumbled management of the National Vulnerability Database](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/01/nist-nvd-management-problems/).
